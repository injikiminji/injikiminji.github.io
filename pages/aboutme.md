My work explores how art moves across institutions, markets, and media, with a focus on the politics of visibility and value. I am committed to supporting artists and expanding the discursive spaces where art is written about, curated, and encountered. <br> <br>
Over the past decade, I have contributed to curatorial and editorial projects with institutions such as Curationist, the Nasher Sculpture Center, the Metropolitan Museum of Art, Miguel Abreu Gallery, and the Pera Museum. <br> <br>
Fluent in English, Korean, and Turkish, I work across languages and geographies to support cross-cultural engagement and nuanced approaches to interpretation. <br> <br>
I hold a Master’s in Art and Museum Studies from Georgetown University and have led university-level study abroad programs in Europe as a Ph.D. student at the University of Washington, Seattle. <br> <br>
